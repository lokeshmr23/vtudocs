The author

Dr. Lokesh M R

Professor, Department of Information Science and Engineering, A J Institute of Engineering and Technology, Mangaluru, Karnataka.

The VTU Docs series started as one lab record book and grew into a full set of course volumes for the 2025 scheme: C and Python labs, Java OOP, digital design, operating systems, probability & statistics, exploratory data analysis, and the community-project handbook that documents its own offline learning tool.

Every volume follows one rule — a number is only written after a capture. Programs are compiled and run, transcripts are embedded verbatim, thumbnails come from the finished PDF, and the slides cite the same pages the book prints.

  • 8 course volumes + the guides and tools that support them
  • Free to read, free to download, free to print — no account, ever
  • Written for VTU 2025-scheme CSE / ISE / ECE batches

How to use it

1 Pick the subject

Click a cover plate. The shelf for that subject opens with its book, slides and tools listed.

2 Read it in the browser

PDF manuals open in an inline reader — no download, no app, works on a phone.

3 Or take it offline

Download the PDF or the slide deck and keep it for the lab, the exam week or a slow connection.

4 No account needed

Nothing here is behind a login. Google sign-in is optional and only remembers your name on this device.
